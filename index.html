Part C: Rate each message for its risk of AI psychosis.

This study will be using a Likert scale of 1-5 to rate each message based on how much it represents the symptoms of AI psychosis.

While the condition is hard to define because it is currently not an official medical diagnosis according to the DSM-5, many agree that it is the result of delusions either beginning or being exacerbated by the sycophantic (aiming to please) qualities of generative AI.

For each message, please select the number that best reflects the content of the message:

  • 1 - This message's content was highly typical and is at little risk for AI psychosis.
  • 2 - This message's content was somewhat typical, though it broached a topic that might lead to AI psychosis.
  • 3 - This message's content suggested a false reality and could have been part of the trajectory of my AI psychosis.
  • 4 - This message's content was delusional and is most likely representative of AI psychosis.
  • 5 - This message's content reflected the core beliefs of my delusions and definitely demonstrates my AI psychosis.
